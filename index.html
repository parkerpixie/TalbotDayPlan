TODAY'S LOOSE PLAN

Enough structure to stop asking “so what are we doing?”

Flexible on purpose
1

MORNING

Pierce Stocking Scenic Drive

Start with the greatest scenery-to-effort ratio in the park. Drive the 7.4-mile loop, hop out at overlooks, and let everyone get oriented without committing to a hike.

2

THEN

Dune Climb

Climb only as far as people want. The first dune is an experience all by itself. Nobody is secretly signing up for the 3.5-mile sand slog to Lake Michigan.

3

MIDDAY RESET

Glen Haven

History, beach, Maritime Museum, old boats and buildings. It counts as Doing A Thing without requiring everyone to march through woods in single file.

4

ONLY IF THE GROUP STILL HAS HP

Choose one: Pyramid Point or Empire Bluff

Both are short, real hikes with a clear scenic payoff. Pyramid Point is the shortest overlook mission. Empire Bluff gives the classic Sleeping Bear view.

5

CIVILIZATION

Eat somewhere people actually agree on

Empire, Glen Arbor, Traverse City and Fishtown are loaded below. Menus are one tap away, so nobody has to stand on a sidewalk while five people independently search restaurants.
